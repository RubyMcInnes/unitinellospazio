Paintings of fear, freedom and life in the city. Uniti Nello Spazio gallery presents a range of artworks created by our community members, capturing an emotion, a story or a moment within our public spaces.

Together, the works reveal how unequal access to urban space is felt in everyday life, and invite all of us to reflect on how our behaviour shapes the way others move through the city.
